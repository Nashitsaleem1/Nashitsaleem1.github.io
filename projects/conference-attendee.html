Why It Matters

Business Impact

For event organizers and marketing operations teams, attendee data usually arrives as deeply nested JSON that is unusable in a CRM without hours of manual reformatting. Automating that transformation into clean, structured Excel output removes a bottleneck that otherwise delays post-event follow-up and lead routing. Because the pipeline handles the nested structure and field mapping consistently, teams can trust the CRM import at any attendee volume, turning a manual export chore into a repeatable step in the workflow.
