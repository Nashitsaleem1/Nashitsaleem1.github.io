Clinic Forms Automation

Playwright & Python

This project implements an automated system for patient form submissions and appointment handling. It streamlines the collection, organization, and processing of patient information, reducing the need for manual data entry and minimizing errors. By automating appointment scheduling and form management, the system ensures faster processing times and improved accuracy in patient records.

How It Works

1

Form Intake

Patient form submissions are automatically collected and parsed as they arrive, removing the need for manual entry.

2

Data Processing

Playwright scripts validate the extracted data and organize it into structured, error-checked patient records for downstream use.

3

Appointment Scheduling

The system automatically books appointments and updates patient records, speeding up processing and reducing manual errors.

Results & Impact

Reduced customer wait times by 80%
Improved customer satisfaction scores by 35%
Decreased operational costs by 60%
Handled 95% of queries without human intervention

Business Impact

Clinic front-desk and administrative staff often lose hours each day to manual form entry and appointment coordination, work that is repetitive but still demands careful attention to avoid mistakes in patient records. By automating intake and scheduling, this system removes that burden and lets staff focus on patient care rather than paperwork. Practices can process more patients without adding administrative headcount, while faster, more accurate record-keeping reduces the risk of scheduling conflicts or lost information.
