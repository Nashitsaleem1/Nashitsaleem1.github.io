How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Scheduled Sheet Scan

Apps Script scans Google Sheets on a recurring schedule, reading each team's goal cycle dates and current deadlines.

2

Deadline Calculation

The system flags any goal cycle reaching its 5-day-before-deadline window and matches it to the responsible manager.

3

Slack Reminder Delivery

A targeted Slack message is sent directly to the manager, keeping goal reviews on schedule without manual follow-up.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For managers responsible for keeping goal cycles on track, manually monitoring spreadsheets and remembering upcoming deadlines is easy to neglect amid other priorities, and missed reviews can quietly stall team accountability. By reading deadlines directly from Google Sheets and pushing timely Slack reminders, the system keeps managers on schedule without anyone having to watch a calendar. That removes hours of manual tracking each week and gives HR confidence that goal reviews are consistently completed on time.
