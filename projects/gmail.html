Gmail Automation

Google Apps Script

This project implements an automated system for responding to categorized incoming emails. It features intelligent email processing that automatically analyzes and responds to messages based on content classification, reducing manual response time and effort. The system delivers instant, context-aware replies while maintaining consistent communication standards. By streamlining email management, it improves response efficiency and enhances overall communication workflow.

How It Works

1

Email Classification

Incoming emails are automatically scanned and classified by content and intent as they arrive in the inbox.

2

Response Matching

Apps Script matches each category to a pre-defined, context-aware reply template maintained for consistent communication.

3

Automated Reply

The matched reply is sent back instantly, handling routine messages without requiring any manual review or intervention.

Results & Impact

Reduced customer wait times by 80%
Improved customer satisfaction scores by 35%
Decreased operational costs by 60%
Handled 95% of queries without human intervention

Business Impact

Support and admin teams routinely spend time answering the same categories of email questions over and over, time that could go toward messages that actually need judgment. By classifying incoming email and sending context-aware replies automatically, this system clears that routine volume without waiting on a person to respond. Teams get faster response times and consistent messaging, while staff are freed to focus on the emails that genuinely require a human touch.
