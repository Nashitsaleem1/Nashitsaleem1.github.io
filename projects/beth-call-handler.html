How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Voice Call Intake

Beth answers the incoming call and captures the patient's intake details, scheduling request, or refill need.

2

Tebra API Data Lookup

N8N and Python workflows query the Tebra API in real time to verify patient records and process the request.

3

Request Logging

Each request is logged as a ticket for staff, and Apps Script records the call in a Google Sheet. SMS confirmations to the patient are available as an option.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

Most of the practice's calls arrive after the workday. Over 39 days, Beth answered 1,746 calls from 863 callers, and 86% of them came in after 5 PM Eastern, with the busiest hour at 10 PM. Those are hours a small practice rarely staffs, so without Beth these callers would reach voicemail and wait until morning. Instead, Beth handled each call in about 39 seconds and logged 1,202 requests for the team to pick up the next day. The practice covers its evening and overnight demand without adding reception shifts.
