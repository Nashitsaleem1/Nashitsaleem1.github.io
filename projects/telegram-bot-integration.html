How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Incoming Message Capture

The Telegram API listens continuously for new messages sent to the bot, capturing sender details the moment they arrive.

2

Automated Contact Matching

A Python-based lookup checks each sender against existing HubSpot contact records, resolving matches without human review.

3

HubSpot Sync & Logging

Matched messages are written into HubSpot as structured activity records, keeping communication history complete and current.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For businesses that rely on Telegram as a customer or sales channel, conversations that live outside the CRM are conversations that get lost. This integration keeps every Telegram exchange automatically tied to the right contact record, giving sales and support teams a complete communication history without asking anyone to copy and paste. The result is less time spent on manual data entry and a lower risk of following up on stale or incomplete information.
