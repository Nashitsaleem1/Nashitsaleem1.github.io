Why It Matters

Business Impact

For e-commerce sellers managing listings across a marketplace, pricing and stock data can drift out of sync the moment it is checked manually, leading to overselling or missed pricing opportunities. Syncing the eBay API directly with Google Sheets removes that manual reconciliation step, giving teams one always-current source of truth instead of juggling multiple spreadsheets. The practical benefit is fewer stock and pricing errors, faster decision-making across departments, and hours of manual updating returned to the team each week.
