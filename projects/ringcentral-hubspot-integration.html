How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Call & SMS Capture

A Python service listens to the RingCentral API for new calls, SMS, and recordings across every extension.

2

AI Summary Generation

Each interaction is processed and paired with an AI-generated summary describing the call or message content.

3

HubSpot Timeline Sync

The enriched record is pushed through the HubSpot API and appears on the contact's timeline in real time.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For sales and support teams, manually logging calls, texts, and recordings into a CRM is tedious and easy to fall behind on, which leaves account histories incomplete right when reps need them most. By automatically syncing every interaction, along with an AI-generated summary, directly into HubSpot's contact timeline, this integration removes that logging burden entirely. Teams get a complete, real-time communication history without extra data entry, making follow-ups faster and more informed.
