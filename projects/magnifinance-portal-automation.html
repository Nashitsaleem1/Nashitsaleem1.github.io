How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Excel Data Ingestion

The system reads structured Excel data containing invoice line items, products, categories, and VAT details for each batch.

2

Automated Portal Login

Playwright securely logs into the Magnifinance portal and locates the matching invoice records without manual input.

3

Multi-Line Invoice Update

Products, categories, and VAT are updated across multi-line invoices, processing 1,000+ invoices a month automatically.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For accounting and finance teams, manually re-entering multi-line invoice details into a portal is slow, repetitive, and prone to costly errors, especially as transaction volume grows. By automating the login, data-matching, and update process end to end, this system removes that bottleneck entirely, letting a small back-office team keep pace with a high volume of invoices without adding headcount. The result is more consistent financial records and hours freed up each week for higher-value accounting work rather than manual data entry.
