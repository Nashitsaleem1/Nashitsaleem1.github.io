How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

SMS Intake via Twilio

Twilio receives the incoming text message and passes its content into the workflow for processing.

2

GPT Intent Classification

GPT reads the message and classifies its intent, determining whether it's a scheduling, email, or task request.

3

Cross-Platform Task Automation

Zapier routes the classified request to the right destination—Notion, calendar, or email—completing the task automatically.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

Turning a quick text message into the right action across email, task lists, and calendars normally means stopping to open several different apps. By using AI to classify intent and route each SMS automatically, this system lets a person or small team manage tasks from wherever they are without manual triage. It cuts down on context-switching and administrative overhead, so more time goes toward the actual work instead of organizing it.
