How It Works

Data Consolidation

Voucher entries from Google Sheets and separate records are pulled together into a single working dataset.

2

Automated Voucher Generation

3

Validation & Tracking

Why It Matters

Business Impact

Manual voucher creation and reconciliation is exactly the kind of repetitive, error-prone work that quietly consumes a finance team's time and puts accuracy at risk during audits. By automating generation, validation, and tracking in the tools the finance team already uses, this system cuts down on data entry hours while producing a more reliable, audit-ready paper trail. The business gains faster month-end reconciliation and fewer discrepancies to chase down later.
