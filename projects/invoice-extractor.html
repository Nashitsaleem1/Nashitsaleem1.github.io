How It Works

Incoming PDF invoices are picked up by the Python pipeline as they arrive, queued for automated data extraction.

2

Hybrid Vision + OCR Extraction

3

Rule-Based Validation & Update

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For finance teams processing large volumes of invoices, manually reading each PDF to identify the issuing company and reconcile it against business records is slow and vulnerable to human error. Combining vision-based AI with OCR to extract and validate that data automatically removes the bulk of that manual review, letting finance staff focus on exceptions rather than routine checks. That means invoices move through the pipeline faster, records stay more accurate, and the process holds up as invoice volume grows.
