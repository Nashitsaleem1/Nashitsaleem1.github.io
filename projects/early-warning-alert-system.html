Why It Matters

Business Impact

For a subscription-based emergency alert service, reliability is the entire product: a delayed or missed notification during a real event directly threatens user trust and retention. Consolidating NOAA, FEMA, and GDACS feeds into one Twilio-driven pipeline, backed by Stripe subscription logic and uptime monitoring, removes the risk of relying on a single data source or manual oversight. The practical payoff is a platform that can grow its subscriber base with confidence, deliver alerts within seconds, and keep paying users protected when it matters most.
