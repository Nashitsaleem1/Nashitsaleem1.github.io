How It Works

A closer look at the automated workflow behind the results

1

Faculty Profile Scraping

BeautifulSoup scrapes faculty pages across university websites, collecting profile and publication data at scale.

2

Research Interest Extraction

Python parses each profile to identify a professor's research focus, isolating the details relevant to outreach.

3

Personalized Email Generation

LangChain and OpenAI draft a tailored outreach email for each professor, aligned to their specific research interests.

Why It Matters

Business Impact

For students navigating graduate school admissions, manually researching every professor's work and writing a personalized note does not scale beyond a handful of applications. Automating faculty research and email drafting lets applicants reach far more programs without sacrificing the personal touch that improves response rates. The result is a repeatable outreach process that turns a time-consuming, one-by-one task into something applicants can run across an entire target list, freeing hours for interview prep and application work.
