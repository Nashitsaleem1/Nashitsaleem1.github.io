Are you available for new projects?

Yes. I take on a limited number of computer vision and deep learning projects alongside my MS research. Send me a short description and we can find out if it's a good fit.

Which frameworks and tools do you work with?

PyTorch and TensorFlow for training, OpenCV for classical vision and stereo geometry, YOLO11 with ByteTrack for detection and tracking, UNet for segmentation, and FastAPI with Docker for serving.

Do you work on agriculture and research problems?

Yes. My main research is computer vision for greenhouse crops: fruit sizing from stereo imagery, water-stress sensing from RGB cameras and yield estimation, supported by a TÜBİTAK-funded project.

Can you deploy models to on-prem hardware?

Yes. I've packaged vision pipelines as Python services for on-prem GPU hardware, reading RTSP camera streams and storing results in S3-compatible storage, shipped with Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD.
