👋 Let's talk * Response time is typically around 12 hours
Are you available for new projects?

Yes. I take on a limited number of projects at a time. If you have a vision or deep learning problem in mind, send me a short description and we can find out if it's a good fit.

Which frameworks and tools do you work with?

Mostly PyTorch and TensorFlow for training, OpenCV for classical vision, and ONNX or TensorRT for deployment. I'm comfortable with Hugging Face models and tools like CVAT for labeling.

Can you deploy models to edge devices?

Yes. I optimize models with quantization and export them for GPUs, Jetson boards and mobile, aiming for real-time speed without giving up accuracy.

How do you handle small or messy datasets?

I start by auditing the data, then use augmentation, transfer learning and active labeling to get the most out of what exists, and I'm upfront about what accuracy is realistic.
