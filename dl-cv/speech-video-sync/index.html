Overview

What this project is

My final-year project at UET Taxila was an AI model for recognising Quranic recitation and synchronising it with the text. It automates a task that used to take about five hours of manual video editing, and now takes about five minutes.

The project became the basis of a peer-reviewed paper at the 4th International Conference on Digital Futures and Transformative Technologies (ICoDT2) in 2024.

Approach

How it works

  1. Recognise

    Speech recognition turns the recitation audio into text.

  2. Align

    Forced alignment matches the text to timestamps in the audio.

  3. Generate

    Automated video generation builds the synchronised video from the aligned text.

  4. Deliver

    A finished video in about five minutes instead of about five hours.
